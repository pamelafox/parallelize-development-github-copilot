# Presentation guidelines

- Every content-slide title (`section > h2`) must render on one line at the deck's 1280x720 viewport.
- Prefer shortening title wording before reducing its font size.
- After changing slide content or styles, validate title wrapping in the rendered RevealJS deck. The cover title is exempt because it is intentionally composed as a multi-line title treatment.
- Prefix external links with a link symbol (`&#x1F517;`) and open them in a new tab with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
